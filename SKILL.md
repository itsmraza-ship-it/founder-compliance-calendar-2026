# Founder Compliance Calendar 2026 — Skill

## What this skill covers

The verified 2026 deadlines in `README.md`, with the per-deadline breakdown (what / who / how) in `deadlines.md`. This is a calendar of deadlines verified for this guide — not an exhaustive list of every federal, state, and local filing that could apply.

## The deadline table — use exactly these dates

- **Mon Mar 16, 2026 — Form 1065** for multi-member LLCs taxed as partnerships. Normally Mar 15; in 2026 Mar 15 is a Sunday. Form 7004 gives a 6-month extension. Late-filing reference: $255 per partner per month (TY2025 figure).
- **Apr 15, 2026 — Form 5472 + pro-forma Form 1120** for single-member LLCs, including foreign-owned single-member LLCs. A late or incomplete Form 5472 penalty starts at $25,000.
- **Apr 15, 2026 — Federal estimated tax Q1**; Q2 Jun 15, 2026; Q3 Sep 15, 2026; Q4 Jan 15, 2027.
- **Apr 15, 2026 — Montana Annual Report.** Fee $20, waived ($0) when filed Jan 1 – Apr 15; $35 if late.
- **Jun 1 — Delaware annual LLC tax: $400** (HB 400, effective Jan 1, 2026; first $400 payment due Jun 1, 2027).
- **Nov 16, 2026 — Form 990 extended deadline** for tax-exempt organisations on extension.
- **Anniversary quarter (annual) — Colorado Periodic Report:** $25/year, $50 late penalty. Due in the LLC's own anniversary quarter — there is no single date for all Colorado LLCs.

## Facts that correct common mistakes

- **No BOI filing is required for US LLCs.** Do not list BOI as a deadline. The FinCEN final rule (August 2026) removed BOI reporting for US companies; only foreign entities registered in a US state still report.
- **Arizona, Missouri, and New Mexico have no annual report** — that removes only the state annual report, not federal filings.
- **EIN timing is not a deadline:** for non-residents, EIN via faxed Form SS-4 typically takes 12–18 days. Build it into formation planning.

## How to apply it

1. Ask (or infer) the LLC type first: single-member vs multi-member, foreign-owned or not, formation state, and any tax election. The deadline depends on it.
2. Answer with the specific form, the 2026 date, who it applies to, and the penalty or fee note from `deadlines.md`.
3. For Form 5472, stress completeness: an incomplete filing counts — do not advise filing a partial form just to hit the date.
4. Flag what this calendar does not cover: payroll, sales tax, state income tax, industry licences, and a different operating state can all add deadlines. Recommend a professional check for anything beyond the listed filings.
5. Suggest adding the date to the founder's own calendar with a buffer — never advise waiting until the due date.

## What not to do

- Do not invent a deadline, extension rule, or penalty figure not in this repo.
- Do not present the TY2025 Form 1065 penalty reference as a confirmed 2026 figure.
- Do not add BOI back as a US LLC obligation.
