# Founder Compliance Calendar 2026

The key 2026 US compliance deadlines for LLC founders — including non-resident and foreign-owned LLCs — in one place.

This is a calendar of deadlines verified for this guide, not an exhaustive list of every federal, state, and local filing that could apply to your business. Your LLC's state, tax elections, payroll, sales tax, and industry can add more.

📄 **Extended version:** see [deadlines.md](deadlines.md) for a per-deadline breakdown of what each filing is, who it applies to, and how it is filed.

## 2026 deadline table

| Date (2026) | Deadline | Who it applies to | Key detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday, March 16, 2026** | Form 1065 — US Return of Partnership Income | Multi-member LLCs taxed as partnerships | Normally due March 15; in 2026 March 15 falls on a Sunday, so the deadline is Monday, March 16, 2026. Form 7004 gives a 6-month extension. Late-filing penalty reference: $255 per partner per month (TY2025 figure) |
| **April 15, 2026** | Form 5472 + pro-forma Form 1120 | Single-member LLCs, including foreign-owned single-member LLCs | Late or incomplete Form 5472 penalty starts at $25,000 |
| **April 15, 2026** | Federal estimated tax — Q1 | Individuals / owners paying estimated tax | Q1 payment due April 15 |
| **April 15, 2026** | Montana Annual Report | Montana LLCs | Fee $20, but **waived ($0)** when filed January 1 – April 15; $35 if filed late |
| **June 1** | Delaware annual LLC tax | Delaware LLCs | Delaware annual LLC tax — $400 (HB 400, effective Jan 1, 2026; first $400 payment due June 1, 2027) |
| **June 15, 2026** | Federal estimated tax — Q2 | Individuals / owners paying estimated tax | Q2 payment due June 15 |
| **September 15, 2026** | Federal estimated tax — Q3 | Individuals / owners paying estimated tax | Q3 payment due September 15 |
| **November 16, 2026** | Form 990 — extended deadline | Tax-exempt organizations on extension | Extended deadline: November 16, 2026 |
| **Anniversary quarter (annual)** | Colorado Periodic Report | Colorado LLCs | $25/year; $50 late penalty. Due in the LLC's anniversary quarter — there is no single calendar date for all Colorado LLCs |
| **January 15, 2027** | Federal estimated tax — Q4 | Individuals / owners paying estimated tax | Q4 payment for the 2026 tax year, due January 15, 2027 |

## States with no annual report

These states have **no annual report requirement** for LLCs:

- **Arizona** — no annual report
- **Missouri** — no annual report
- **New Mexico** — no annual report

No annual report does **not** mean no obligations at all — federal tax filings and other state requirements can still apply.

## BOI — you do NOT file it for a US LLC

> **US LLCs do NOT file Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI).**

The FinCEN final rule (August 2026) removed BOI reporting for US companies. BOI is therefore **not** listed as a deadline in this calendar, and a US LLC should not treat it as one.

## Notes — not deadlines

### EIN for non-residents

An EIN is obtained with Form SS-4. For non-residents, the SS-4 is submitted by **fax** and typically takes **12–18 days**. This is a processing-time note for when you form or need to file — it is not a calendar deadline.

## A note on penalties

- **Form 5472:** a late or incomplete Form 5472 penalty starts at **$25,000**. This is the single most expensive routine mistake for foreign-owned single-member LLCs.
- **Form 1065:** the late-filing penalty reference used in this guide is **$255 per partner per month (TY2025 figure)**.
- **Colorado Periodic Report:** **$50** late penalty.
- **Montana Annual Report:** **$35** if filed late (the standard $20 fee is waived to $0 when filed January 1 – April 15).
