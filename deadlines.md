# 2026 Compliance Deadlines — Extended Guide

The full, per-deadline version of the [Founder Compliance Calendar 2026](README.md).

For each deadline: **what** it is, **who** must file, and **how** it works. Only deadlines verified for this guide are included — this is not an exhaustive list of every filing that could apply to a given business.

---

## March 16, 2026 — Form 1065 (multi-member LLCs)

**What:** Form 1065, US Return of Partnership Income — the annual federal information return for a partnership-taxed business.

**Who:** Multi-member LLCs taxed as partnerships.

**How / key facts:**

- Normally due **March 15**.
- In 2026, March 15 falls on a **Sunday**, so the deadline is **Monday, March 16, 2026**.
- **Form 7004** gives a **6-month extension** to file.
- Late-filing penalty reference: **$255 per partner per month (TY2025 figure)**.
- File Form 1065 with the IRS; if you need more time, file Form 7004 by the original deadline.

---

## April 15, 2026 — Form 5472 + pro-forma Form 1120 (single-member LLCs)

**What:** Form 5472, Information Return of a 25% Foreign-Owned US Corporation or a Foreign Corporation Engaged in a US Trade or Business, filed together with a pro-forma Form 1120.

**Who:** Single-member LLCs — **including foreign-owned single-member LLCs**. This filing applies even in the common case where the LLC has had no US income to report on a regular return.

**How / key facts:**

- Due **April 15**.
- The Form 5472 is attached to the pro-forma Form 1120 and filed as that combined package.
- A **late or incomplete Form 5472 penalty starts at $25,000**. Incomplete counts — do not file a partial or blank 5472 to "meet the date."
- Accuracy matters more than almost any other routine LLC filing: reportable transactions with the foreign owner / related parties are the core of the form.

## April 15, 2026 — Federal estimated tax, Q1

**What:** First-quarter federal estimated tax payment for the 2026 tax year.

**Who:** Individuals / owners who pay federal tax through estimated payments rather than withholding.

**How / key facts:**

- Q1 is due **April 15, 2026**.
- The remaining 2026 quarters are listed in their own sections below: Q2 on June 15, Q3 on September 15, and Q4 on January 15, 2027.

## April 15, 2026 — Montana Annual Report

**What:** The Montana Secretary of State Annual Report that keeps a Montana LLC in good standing.

**Who:** Montana LLCs.

**How / key facts:**

- Due **April 15**.
- Standard fee is **$20**, but the fee is **waived ($0) when filed January 1 – April 15**.
- If filed late, the fee is **$35**.
- Filing inside the January 1 – April 15 window is both on time and free.

## June 1 — Delaware annual LLC tax

**What:** Delaware's flat annual tax for LLCs. Delaware LLCs pay this annual tax; it is not an annual report.

**Who:** Delaware LLCs.

**How / key facts:**

- Due **June 1**.
- **Delaware annual LLC tax — $400 (HB 400, effective Jan 1, 2026; first $400 payment due June 1, 2027).**
- HB 400 raised the amount from **$300** to **$400**, effective **January 1, 2026**.

## Anniversary quarter (annual) — Colorado Periodic Report

**What:** Colorado's Periodic Report — the recurring state report that keeps a Colorado LLC in good standing.

**Who:** Colorado LLCs.

**How / key facts:**

- **Annual**, due in the LLC's **anniversary quarter** — the timing follows your LLC's formation anniversary, not a single date that applies to every Colorado LLC.
- Fee: **$25/year**.
- Late penalty: **$50**.
- Look up your LLC's own anniversary quarter and file inside it; do not rely on a generic calendar date.

## States with no annual report

**What:** Some states impose no annual report requirement on LLCs at all.

**Who / which states:**

- **Arizona** — no annual report requirement
- **Missouri** — no annual report requirement
- **New Mexico** — no annual report requirement

**How / key facts:**

- There is no annual report to file (and no annual report fee to pay) in these states.
- This does not remove federal tax filings or any other obligation that applies to your LLC — it removes only the state annual report.

---

## BOI — not a deadline for US LLCs

**What:** Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) reporting.

**Who:** **US LLCs do NOT file BOI.**

**How / key facts:**

- The **FinCEN final rule (August 2026)** removed BOI reporting for US companies.
- BOI is deliberately **not** listed as a deadline anywhere in this calendar. Do not add it back as one for a US LLC.

## Notes — processing times, not deadlines

### EIN for non-residents (Form SS-4 by fax)

- An EIN is obtained with Form SS-4.
- For non-residents, the SS-4 is submitted by **fax**.
- Processing typically takes **12–18 days**.
- This is included as planning information for formation and filing — it is **not** a calendar deadline.
